# Hiring Board Course Context and Venture Template

Fill in the two sections below, then run the board command in your tool. The board reviews your NEXT HIRE and your org design, then hands you the right next hire, a scorecard for it, and the hire you're rationalizing.

---

## 1) COURSE CONTEXT (delete this section if you're a founder running it for yourself)
You are helping a student in an entrepreneurship course. They are the founder of an early-stage venture deciding who to hire next. Keep the feedback direct and practical. Speak to the student as the founder. No corporate hedging.

## 2) YOUR VENTURE AND THE HIRE
- Company and what it does: [one honest paragraph, no pitch language]
- Stage: [pre-revenue / early revenue / scaling]
- Current headcount and who owns what: [list every person and their area, even if it's just you]
- The hire you think you need next: [title, level (junior IC / senior IC / manager / exec), why now, what they'd own]
- What "great in 90 days" looks like for this role: [or write "not defined"]
- Your ranked "who I need to hire next" list: [the next 2-4 roles, in fill order]
- Your own plate, answer honestly:
   - What only you can do right now: [ ]
   - What you do that drains you or you're bad at: [ ]
   - What you avoid: [ ]
- What actually breaks if you DON'T make this hire this quarter: [be concrete]
- Money reality: [runway in months, rough monthly burn, can you afford market comp for an A-player here, mark guesses]
- A past hire that didn't work out, and why (optional): [ ]
- What you're worried about (optional): [ ]
