# Pricing Boardroom: Course Context Template

Your instructor fills this in once. Save it as `~/open-boardrooms/course-context.md` and the board reads it at the start of every run, so the advisors hold your venture to this class's standards. If you are a founder running the board for yourself, skip this file. The board runs without it.

The board collects your venture, your numbers, and your price in its own intake when you type `/pricingboard`. Have them ready: what you charge today and per what unit, how you set it, what the customer does instead, how many people have actually paid, what one more unit costs you to deliver, and the price you are afraid to charge. Write GUESS or UNKNOWN where you do not know a figure. Never a made-up number.

---

COURSE CONTEXT (calibrate the board to this class):
- Course and level: [your instructor fills this in, or leave blank]
- Stage of our ventures: [idea / first sales / recurring revenue]
- What counts as a real price here: [e.g. a number someone outside the class has paid]
- Non-negotiables: [e.g. no price set from cost alone, no "students can't pay," every
  number has a unit]
- Constraints: [e.g. no budget, one semester, team of 3]

---

The non-negotiables line is the one that changes the run. Whatever you write there, the advisors enforce by name in every round, and a student who breaks one hears about it from five people at once.
