# Kill Board Course Context and Venture Template

Fill in the two sections below, then run the board command in your tool. The board attacks your own case for continuing, then hands you a committed verdict (DOUBLE DOWN, PIVOT, or KILL), a three-tripwire kill criteria contract with dates, and the payload that comes with the verdict: what compounds, what lives, or what you keep.

---

## 1) COURSE CONTEXT (delete this section if you're a founder running it for yourself)
You are helping a student in an entrepreneurship course. They are the founder of a venture deciding whether it should continue. Keep the feedback direct and practical. Speak to the student as the founder. The verdict lands on the venture, never on the student.
