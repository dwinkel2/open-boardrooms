# Example run: the AI Policy Boardroom on a three-sentence syllabus policy

What a teacher gets back when they paste the setup sheet into a chat. The policy below is the generic "responsibly and ethically" paragraph that sits in many syllabi, placed in an invented sophomore entrepreneurship course for this test, not a real instructor's syllabus or a real institution's rules. The run went through the policy spec, Round 1 (teardown), Round 2 (the fight and the fork), Round 3 (non-negotiables, the enforcement audit, the rebuild, and two second passes), and the three deliverables. The user typed "continue" between rounds.

Run on 2026-11-02 with Claude Sonnet. Output is unedited.

## 1. THE RULES LAWYER

**OVERALL VERDICT: COLLAPSES**

**THE MOMENT IT BREAKS:** *"AI may support your learning but may not replace your own thinking."*

Week 3, the first case memo. I paste the case into an AI, get a draft, and rewrite every sentence in my own words. My thinking was *supported*. The AI never *replaced* anything, because I read it, edited it, and submitted it. Show me the sentence that stops me. There isn't one. "Replace" has no measurable meaning. You email me "this feels like replacement." I reply that my thinking was in the editing. **Clause ratings:**

**Top 3 fixes:**

**Fix 1: Define the prohibited act by what the student must still produce, not by a verb.**
- *Hole:* "may not replace your own thinking."
- *Scenario:* above. Every prohibited use can be reframed as support.
- *Rewrite:* "Every submission must contain judgments, choices, and conclusions you can explain out loud without looking at the screen. Anything you can't explain is not yours to submit."
- *Enforcement cost:* a 60-second oral check on a sampled submission. You already said you'd spend a minute per submission.

**Fix 2: Make the interview rule a hard line.**
- *Hole:* nothing in the policy touches your most important rule.
- *Scenario:* last spring's fabricated interviewee, again, with no sentence to stop it.
- *Rewrite:* "The Problem Discovery Memo requires a real conversation with a real person. Invented, composed, or AI-simulated interview material is fabrication, whatever tool produced it."

**Fix 3: Name the process before the penalty.**
- *Hole:* "Violations will be handled under the university's academic integrity policy."
- *Scenario:* I get flagged, and the policy says nothing about what happens first. I argue the process itself was unfair.
- *Rewrite:* "If I have a concern, I'll ask you to walk me through your work before any grade changes."

> **AI in This Course**
>
> **Why.** Entrepreneurs use AI daily, and you'll be expected to. This course teaches you to use it to pressure-test your thinking, not replace it. The one place it can never stand in is the interview: a made-up customer produces a made-up business.
