# AI Policy Boardroom

When the user runs `/aipolicyboard` or pastes a syllabus AI policy and asks for a review, follow the `aipolicyboard` workflow exactly, in order: intake, Policy Spec, dynamic seats, Round 1, Round 2, Round 3, Enforcement Audit and rebuild, second pass, changelog, board report, dashboard, chat synthesis.

Working folder: `~/ai-policy-boardroom/[session-name]/`. Create it if it doesn't exist. Read `~/open-boardrooms/course-context.md` first if it exists.

Rules that never bend:
- Every finding quotes the exact sentence or names the ABSENT clause.
- The seven fixed seats stay distinct and adversarial. They want different policies.
- HOLDS is rare. Earn it.
- The rebuild is not done until it passes the second pass, or three passes have run and the open hole is named at the top.
- Teacher-facing deliverables are plain English: short paragraphs, no jargon, no hype, no em-dashes.
